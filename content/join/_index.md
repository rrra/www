Membership in Red River Radio Amateurs is open to anyone interested in
the hobby whether or not they currently hold an Amateur Radio license.

### Follow These Steps To Become A Club Member

**It's as easy as 1 -- 2 -- 3 ...**

1 --- Send in your membership application
: Complete the [fillable PDF RRRA membership application](/application)
and either

* :star: E-mail the form to the 
{{< mailto id="treasurer" subject="RRRA Membership Application" >}}.
<br>**OR**<br>
* Print the form and bring it to the [next club meeting](/dates/club-meetings)

2 --- Pay your dues
: The current membership dues are listed on our
[membership application](/application) and our
[pay by credit, debit, PayPal page]({{< relref "/join/pay-by-card.md" >}}).
These forms of payment are accepted:

* :star: [Credit, Debit, PayPal]({{< relref "/join/pay-by-card.md" >}})
* [Check]({{< relref "/join/pay-by-check.md" >}})
* [Cash](/join/pay-by-cash)

3 --- Finish the membership process
: :bangbang: New members **MUST** visit our [on boarding page]({{< relref "/about/onboarding.md" >}})
to find out how to complete the membership process:

* :star: Subscribe to the RRRA mailing list --- our primary form of club
communication.
* :star: Request a groupware account for access to members only content.

:warning: Annual membership renewals are due at the
[January Club Meeting](/dates/club-meetings/).
