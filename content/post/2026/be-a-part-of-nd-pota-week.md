Celebrate the anniversary of North Dakota statehood---November 2, 1889---with
the 3rd annual North Dakota Parks on the Air (POTA) event from November 1st to
the 7th, 2026.
<!--more-->

What started as a way to commemorate North Dakota statehood has become
an annual activity for hams throughout the state. The idea is simple:
get out, activate our parks, put North Dakota on the air, and give POTA
hunters around the country---and the world---a full week to work a
state that can sometimes be tough to find.

Activate POTA as you normally would between November 1st and 7th, 2026.
Activate one park or several, operate for an hour or a day or make a
full week of it.

event.
