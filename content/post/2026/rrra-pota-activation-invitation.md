Enjoy a fall [Parks On The Air][POTA] activation while participating in
[North Dakota POTA Week][week]!

Join RRRA at their group operating event for North Dakota POTA week
from [Fort Abercrombie State Historic Site][site] in Abercrombie, ND.
<!--more-->

All hams are welcome to stop by and operate and see what Parks On The
Air is all about. Participants will be able to operate the club callsign
(W0ILO) or operate on their own from the site to count as their own POTA
activation. There are no awards for North Dakota POTA Week; the only
objective is to get on the air and ***have fun***.

We will officially be operating starting at 10:30 AM, but hams are
welcome to arrive early and start operating on their own. This is an
informal event and the only goal is to get on the air.

Please note there will be no public restrooms available and services in
Abercrombie are limited (i.e. plan accordingly; if you want something
bring it).

Talk-in will be on 146.520 MHz (the location is just outside reliable
repeater range).

{{< banner >}}
In the event of inclement weather this event will be cancelled. Notice
of cancellation will be sent to the RRRA
[Club Announcements](https://lists.rrra.org/mailman/listinfo/announce) and
[Club Discussions](https://lists.rrra.org/mailman/listinfo/rrr) mailing lists.
